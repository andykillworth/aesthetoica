About

æsthetoica is a small art project by Andy Killworth. I live a few miles from Lyme Regis in west Dorset.

Every day the site marbles a sheet of paper using things that pass through Lyme Regis without anyone noticing: cosmic rays, small shifts in the Earth’s magnetic field, meteors overhead, gravitational waves from colliding black holes, the hum of the National Grid and edits to obscure Wikipedia articles. The feeds are real and arrive as they happen. At midnight the day’s sheet is pulled, given a number and added to the archive.

The same data can also be seen as a cloud chamber or as pens on a smoked drum. Use the switcher at the top of the tray.

What is true and what is not

Thomasin Gale, the bookbinder of Broad Street, is made up, and so is the history of the word. Her nightly ledger line is written by a language model from that day’s readings. The readings are real.
